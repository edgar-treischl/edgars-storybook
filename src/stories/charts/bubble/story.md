## Basic

Bubble charts display relationships between two numerical variables using the x- and y-axes, while bubble size represents a third variable. Colors can encode additional categories, making it possible to compare multiple dimensions and identify patterns, clusters, and outliers in a single visualization.

The Gapminder bubble chart illustrates the relationship between GDP per capita and life expectancy across countries in 2007. Bubble sizes represent population, and colors distinguish continents. It highlights the general association between greater national wealth and longer life expectancy, while revealing differences between countries and regions.
